How the classification is calculated

Each process component is scored 0, 1 or 2. The process percentage is the total points achieved divided by the maximum possible points for that skill.

The product percentage is calculated as successful outcomes ÷ total attempts × 100.

The integrated score weights Process at 60% and Product at 40%:

Integrated score = (Process × 0.60) + (Product × 0.40)

Initial: below 50%
Emerging: 50–74%
Proficient: 75% or above

This is a transparent rule-based judgement designed to support, not replace, professional observation.
